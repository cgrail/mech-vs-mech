# What's New — 1.2

App Store release notes for MECH VS MECH 1.2, covering everything since 1.0.
Plain text below the line; paste it into App Store Connect as-is.

---

Everything since 1.0 — night fighting, capture the flag, room-run lobbies, and a district with a hole in it.

NIGHT MODE
• The district is now fought at night by default. Your mech carries a sensor lamp, and enemy mechs and turrets exist only where your sensors reach them.
• Contact fades instead of popping: half a mech behind a wall is half a silhouette, and one that ducks behind a pillar never fully disappears.
• Take damage and the lamp browns out and narrows with your hull, then comes back as you self-repair.
• Turrets you build carry their own lamp, so a defended corner stays readable after you walk away. Enemy turrets get none.
• DAY MODE is one row away in the mission menu, and a lobby room fights in the weather its creator picked.

CAPTURE THE FLAG
• A second mode beside base assault. Walk into the enemy flag inside their compound, carry it back to your own stand, three captures take the district.
• Dying drops the flag where you fell, touching your own dropped flag sends it home, and an untouched flag returns on its own after 25 seconds.
• Flags decide the match and nothing else — levelling the enemy base only cuts off their reinforcements.

JUMP JETS AND MOVEMENT
• A new ⬆️ button jumps your mech a full terrain tier: high ground is reachable anywhere and a pit is no longer a one-way trap. Walls still stop you.
• Push the stick to its rim to run. The knob turns gold while you are sprinting.
• Damage now costs speed — down to 65% at zero hull, and back up as you repair, so a fight you are losing is one you cannot stroll out of.
• Legs shuffle sideways when you strafe instead of marching forward on the spot, and the walk is driven by ground actually covered, so a mech pinned against a wall stands still like one.
• A turret you build now lands beside you instead of across your path.

MULTIPLAYER
• Phones and browsers share the same lobby and the same match.
• The room's creator picks the district, the mode and the weather, and the map comes from the server — so nobody in a room can end up on a different map.
• The map picker is the level select's own list, map pictures and all.
• If there is exactly one room going with space in it, you walk straight into it: callsign, teams, deploy.
• A callsign somebody already has gets a number (ACE 2) instead of a refusal.
• START MATCH is the only button that matters now — every pilot deploys as their match lands.
• The result screen rolls on to the next district by itself after ten seconds; NEXT MAP skips the wait, BACK TO LOBBY gets you out. If everyone else has left, it returns you to the lobby instead.

DISTRICTS
• Three new ones, including THE RIFT: a chasm splitting the map in two with three bridges across it and no floor anywhere else. Step off and you keep falling.
• THE SPRAWL and THE EXPANSE are the largest districts in the game, built for 5v5.
• Win a district and you drop straight into the next one instead of returning to the menu.

ENEMY MECHS
• They follow a wall around instead of grinding into it, probe with their real width so a shoulder no longer clips a corner, and jump ledges to reach you.
• A mech that can see you keeps its guns on you while it side-steps.

LOOK AND FEEL
• Filmic tone mapping and bloom, a dusk gradient sky the distance fog takes its colour from, panelled walls, muzzle flashes on every gun, and a cockpit vignette.
• Menus rebuilt as titled cards over the orbiting district: the way back pinned at the top, one action at the bottom, and a picture of every map drawn from the map itself.
• The same layout fits an iPhone SE and a Pro Max.
• Entering a match no longer stalls the menu — music, map pictures and the long district lists are off the main thread.

FIXES
• A flag carried by a mech that dies is now dropped where it fell.
• A flag dropped over a chasm goes home instead of being lost.
• Rolling into a rematch no longer reports you to the other pilots as having left.
